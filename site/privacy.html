Privacy notice

How this website handles your information

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Who we are

Lia 97 is an AI & software company based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. For any privacy question, email info@lia97.com.

The free evaluation form

When you submit the form, WhatsApp opens with a message to us containing what you typed: your name, business name, phone number, industry, the service you need and any details you add. Nothing is sent to us until you send that message yourself in WhatsApp, and WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to it.

The AI chat

If you use the AI chat, the messages you type are sent to our AI provider, MiniMax, so it can reply to you. Please do not type sensitive personal information in the chat. If you share a website address, our server opens that public website to check it and sends the results of those checks (not the page itself) to MiniMax. We keep each conversation for 7 days. If you ask for your quote or a call back, the name and phone number or email you enter, together with the conversation, are emailed to us so we can reply to you. We use this only to reply to you.

Analytics and cookies

This website does not currently use analytics or tracking cookies. If we add analytics tools such as Google Analytics or Microsoft Clarity, they will load only after you choose “Allow analytics”, and we will update this notice first.

Information stored in your browser

  • If you arrive through a campaign link, the campaign details are kept in your browser for that visit (session storage) so that, if you allow analytics, we can see which campaign brought you.
  • If you make an analytics choice, that choice is saved in your browser (local storage) so we do not ask again.
  • Your language (English or Arabic) and light or dark mode choice are saved in your browser (local storage) so the site remembers them.

This information stays on your device. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

How we use your enquiry

We use the details you send us only to reply to you and to discuss your project. We do not sell your information and we do not add you to mailing lists.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing info@lia97.com.
