# Lia97 — Brand & Company Profile

## Brand foundation

**Brand promise:** Systems that move business.

**Positioning:** Lia97 is the Dubai technology partner behind the whole system—connecting AI, operations, digital experience, data, and cloud infrastructure.

**Mission:** Turn business complexity into technology that creates measurable leverage.

**Vision:** Make connected, intelligent systems accessible to the companies shaping tomorrow.

**Audience:** UAE and GCC startups, growth companies, established businesses modernizing operations, and internal transformation teams.

## Company profile

- **Base:** Dubai, United Arab Emirates
- **Markets:** UAE, GCC, and global delivery
- **Core services:** AI agents and automation; CRM and business systems; websites and commerce; mobile apps and platforms; cloud systems, APIs, and integrations
- **Delivery model:** Strategy → Design → Build → Integrate → Scale
- **Engagement models:** Product builds, system modernization, and ongoing technology partnership

## Verbal identity

**Primary tagline:** Systems that move business.

**Elevator pitch:** Lia97 builds AI agents, CRM platforms, websites, apps, API integrations, and connected technology systems for ambitious companies. From strategy and product design to engineering, integration, and scale, one senior team owns the whole system.

**Short bio:** Dubai technology studio building AI, software, and connected business systems.

**Tone:** Clear, direct, intelligent, confident, and human. Explain complexity without hiding behind it. Prefer useful specifics over hype.

## Brand principles

1. **Precise:** Every decision has a reason. Every interface and system has a job.
2. **Connected:** Products, data, teams, and channels should operate as one system.
3. **Ambitious:** Build for the company's next stage, not only today's requirement.
4. **Owned:** Stay accountable from early framing through launch and evolution.

## Visual identity

- **Obsidian:** `#080B0A` — primary environment
- **Calm blue:** `#3E5BD8` (light mode) / `#8AA2FF` (dark mode) — action and connection (the one accent, since the Oct 2026 redesign; electric lime `#B8FF3D` was the earlier accent)
- **Warm white:** `#F4F5EF` — clarity and typography
- **Slate:** `#87918B` — supporting text

Since the October 2026 redesign the website is light by default (soft grey `#ECECE8` and white surfaces, near-black text) with a dark mode (charcoal `#111314`), one calm blue accent, Manrope type (IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic), thin hairlines, a giant thin "1997" and the owner's logo (a new Lia97 logo is on its way; the company was renamed from 1997 Labs in Nov 2026). Keep layouts spacious and legible. The 1997 monogram remains the compact mark.

Always show the descriptor **AI & Software Company** next to the name, so every visitor knows at a glance that Lia97 is a technology company.

## Messaging pillars

### Intelligence that works
AI agents and automation should move real work forward, not remain demonstrations.

### Operations that connect
CRM, dashboards, data, and integrations should reflect how the business actually operates.

### Experiences that convert
Websites, commerce, and apps should turn attention into trust, action, and useful customer relationships.

### Platforms that compound
Cloud architecture and APIs should stay secure, maintainable, and ready for growth.

## Boilerplate

Lia97 is a Dubai-based technology company building AI agents, CRM platforms, websites, apps, API integrations, and connected business systems. The studio combines strategy, product design, engineering, automation, and integration to help ambitious companies move faster with technology that stays useful.
